Method

Box-Cox transform → linear mixed-effects model, GA as linear or quadratic by BIC, random intercept per fetus → percentiles as predicted mean + z·residual SD, back-transformed.

Reference curves built on the AGA (appropriately-grown) cohort only, BCM site, GA 16-40 weeks.
